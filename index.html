Welcome to Sakani

Sakani is a website for the female students' housing at Sultan Qaboos University. It brings the daily housing services together in one place, so students can do them from their rooms, and supervisors can follow their units easily.

What You Can Do with Sakani

  • Nightly Check-in - sign in for the 9:00 PM check-in from your room.
  • Study Room Booking - book a study room at a free time.
  • Laundry Booking - choose a laundry time without waiting.
  • Bus Schedule - see the bus routes and times for your housing complex.
  • Room Change - view available rooms and send a change request.
  • Supervisor Dashboard - supervisors follow check-in and requests.

How to Start

  1. Log in with your student account.
  2. Choose the service you need from the menu.
  3. Fill in the form and submit it.
