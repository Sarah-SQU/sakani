All buses start and end at the meeting point: College of Arts and Buildings E and F

Bus Routes

From the meeting point, the buses split into two routes. The College of Law and the Main Library are shared stops, so you can take either route from there.

Green Route

  1. College of Science
  2. College of Engineering
  3. College of Law
  4. Main Library
  5. Housing Complex 4
  6. Housing Complex 5
  7. Back to the meeting point

Red Route

  1. Housing Complex 1
  2. Housing Complex 2
  3. Housing Complex 3 and College of Nursing
  4. College of Law
  5. Main Library
  6. College of Medicine
  7. College of Education and College of Agriculture
  8. Back to the meeting point

Bus Tips

  • Be at the bus stop 5 minutes before the bus arrives.
  • Keep your student ID card with you.
  • Check the route color before you get on the bus.

Bus times may change during exams and holidays.
